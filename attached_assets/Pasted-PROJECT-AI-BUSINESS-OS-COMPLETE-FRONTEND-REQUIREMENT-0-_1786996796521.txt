The platform should not feel like a simple chatbot.

It should feel like:

A complete AI-powered business team working under the business owner's supervision.

The AI should:

Understand → Analyze → Recommend → Create → Automate → Execute → Measure → Improve

Important:

Routine, low-risk tasks can be automated.
Important/high-risk actions require human approval.
Every AI action should be visible and auditable.
