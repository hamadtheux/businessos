. The Onboarding Wizard (Crucial)
When the app loads and there is no business configured, it must show a full-screen, step-by-step onboarding wizard.

Step 1: Welcome & Business Basics. (Business Name, Industry dropdown [Farm/Agriculture, Real Estate, E-commerce, Dental, Other], Website, Location, Timezone, Currency).

Step 2: Products & Services. (Dynamic list builder to add products/services with Name, Price, and Stock/Availability).

Step 3: Brand Voice. (Text area for business description, tone of voice, and keywords to avoid).

Step 4: Connect Channels. (Visual cards for WhatsApp, Instagram, Email, Stripe. Include 'Connect' buttons. For the prototype, these can just toggle to 'Connected' state).

Step 5: AI Team Setup. (Show a loading/progress bar that visually builds the 5 AI agents: Business Manager, CMO, Sales, Support, Operations. Then show a success screen: "Your AI Business Team is ready!").

Note: The UI must have a progress indicator at the top of the wizard showing the current step.

2. Dynamic Dashboard Logic (Business Brain)
The dashboard must NOT be hardcoded. It must reflect the onboarding data.

Dynamic Header: "Good Morning, [Business Name]" (e.g., if I enter 'Green Valley Farms', it shows that).

Dynamic Industry Sidebar: If the industry is 'Real Estate', show a sidebar item for 'Properties/Listings'. If it is 'E-commerce', show 'Products'. If it is 'Farm/Agriculture', show 'Inventory/Harvest'.

Dynamic AI Recommendations: Use mock logic. If the industry is 'Farm', show a recommendation like: "Your organic tomatoes are trending this week. Create a post."

Business Switcher: In the Topbar, add a dropdown to switch between multiple businesses. Include a second mock business called "ABC Real Estate" to demonstrate this. When clicked, the dashboard should reload with Real Estate data (Orange/Blue color scheme instead of Green).

3. Color Theming per Business
For 'Green Valley Farms': Use the Green palette (#15803D).

For 'ABC Real Estate': Use a Deep Navy Blue palette (#1E3A8A).

The logo/initials in the sidebar should also change color based on the active business.

4. Required Main Screens (Beyond Dashboard)
Please ensure the routing/navigation is set up for these specific screens and populate them with the industry-specific mock data from 'Green Valley Farms':

/dashboard (The dynamic dashboard described above)

/onboarding (The 5-step wizard described above)

/command (The AI Command Center. A large chat interface asking "What should your AI team do today?" with example prompt chips at the bottom).

/conversations (The 3-column Unified Inbox. Left: Channels. Middle: Chat list. Right: Chat window with AI banner).

/orders (Orders table with filters and mock farm orders).

/customers (Customer database table).

/cmo (AI Marketing Manager. Content calendar + Post generator).

/agents (Grid of AI Agent cards showing 'Active' status).

/approvals (List of pending AI actions with Approve/Reject buttons).

/analytics (Charts using Recharts).

/integrations (Grid of tools with Connect toggles).

/settings (Tabs for Business Profile, Brand Voice, and Team).

Goal: I want to see the full journey from registering a business -> configuring the AI team -> landing on a perfectly tailored business dashboard."
